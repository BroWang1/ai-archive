Takedown Policy

AIArchive preserves open-weight AI models. Public mirrors are limited to models whose published license permits redistribution (e.g. MIT, Apache-2.0); every mirror is byte-identical to the original, credits its creators, and carries the original license verbatim. Per-file integrity manifests are public in the archive repository.

Requesting removal

If you hold rights to material in this archive and believe it is hosted in error, contact us with: (1) the exact URL(s), (2) the work you hold rights to, (3) your good-faith basis for the request, and (4) your contact information.

  • Preferred: open an issue on the archive repository
  • Email: dmca@aiarchive.info

We respond promptly. Where a request is valid, the material is removed or moved to dark storage (retained but not distributed). Where the published license authorizes our copy, we will say so and provide the license text and its capture fingerprint.

Counter-notice

If your material is removed and you believe the removal was mistaken, send the same contact points a statement identifying the material, your good-faith basis, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the applicable federal court, consistent with 17 U.S.C. § 512(g).

Mirrors on Hugging Face are additionally subject to Hugging Face's own DMCA process. This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
